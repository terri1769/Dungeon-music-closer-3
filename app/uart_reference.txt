Communication Protocol (UART Reference)
Use these strings in your write and read functions to keep the two boards synced.
Command          (Direction)      Meaning
ID:[HEX]         (Pico > Pi)      NFC Tag scanned.
VOL:[0-100]      (Pico > Pi)      Knob turned to specific percentage.
CMD:BIG_BUTTON   (Pico > Pi)      Shielded button pressed.
SET_HALO:[MODE]  (Pi > Pico)      Change LED behavior (NORMAL, RED_PULSE, etc.).
AUTH_PASS        (Pi > Pico)      Trigger Green Flash.
AUTH_FAIL        (Pi > Pico)      Trigger Red Flash.
SYNC_VOL:[0-100] (Pi > Pico)      Match Halo to software volume (during ducking).
